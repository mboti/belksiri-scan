Support

Contact: mboti.dev@gmail.com

Contact

For a question, a problem or a suggestion about Belksiri Scan, write to mboti.dev@gmail.com. Please mention your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac), its system version and the application version shown in Settings › About.

On a Windows PC, please mention your Windows version and the application version.

Belksiri Scan Pro

iPhone, iPad and Mac

  • Buy: open the Pro offer (the Pro button, or Settings › Upgrade to Pro), then Buy. The price is the one shown by the App Store.
  • Restore: on another iPhone, iPad or Mac signed in with the same Apple Account, or after reinstalling, open the Pro offer and choose Restore purchases.
  • Waiting for approval: if the purchase needs approval (Ask to Buy) or a payment confirmation, Pro is activated as soon as the App Store confirms it.
  • Refund: refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Windows

  • Buy: open the Pro offer (the Pro button, or Settings › Upgrade to Pro), then Buy. The purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store, which shows the price.
  • Restore: on another Windows PC using the same Microsoft account in the Microsoft Store, or after reinstalling, open the Pro offer and choose Restore purchases.
  • Waiting for confirmation: Pro is activated as soon as the Microsoft Store confirms the licence.
  • Refund: refunds are handled by Microsoft.
  • Apple and Windows are separate: a purchase made on the App Store does not carry over to Windows, and a purchase made on the Microsoft Store does not carry over to iPhone, iPad or Mac.

Preparing the ESP32-C5 board

On iPhone and iPad, Belksiri Scan needs an ESP32-C5 board with its firmware installed. Install it over USB from a Mac or a Windows PC with Belksiri Firmware Installer: download and instructions.

On a Windows PC, the board is optional: the PC scans Wi-Fi itself with its Wi-Fi adapter. If Windows refuses the scan, check Settings › Privacy & security › Location. A board with at least 4 MB of flash can serve as an external measurement source over Bluetooth LE. There are two ways to install its firmware: from Belksiri Scan over USB, or with Belksiri Firmware Installer, a free standalone utility that prepares a board without Belksiri Scan. The installer’s Windows x64 version is an unsigned preview: see the download page for what has been tested.
