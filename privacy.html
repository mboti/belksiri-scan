Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026
Applies to: Belksiri Scan for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows
Published by: Belksiri, an independent developer in France — see section J
Contact: mboti.dev@gmail.com

In short

Belksiri Scan measures and diagnoses Wi-Fi networks. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting. Belksiri runs no server for it and receives none of your data: your projects, measurements and settings stay on your device unless you export or share them yourself.

Some features need to talk to the Internet to measure it. When you use them, your device connects to public services run by other companies (section D). Those connections are the test itself: they carry no information about you beyond what any Internet connection carries, such as your IP address.

Passages marked “iPhone, iPad and Mac” or “Windows” apply only to those devices; the rest of this policy applies to all of them.

A. What stays on your device

The application keeps the following in its own storage on your device. Belksiri has no copy of any of it.

  • Survey projects: floor plans (photos or images you choose), the points you measure, the access points you place, and the measurements taken at each point.
  • Wi-Fi scan results: network names (SSID), access point identifiers (BSSID), signal strength, channel, security and the manufacturer name, found in a database built into the application, offline.
  • Your settings, the last network name used, and the lists of boards and networks you have connected to (name, identifier, firmware version, date and number of uses).
  • Wi-Fi passwords, only if you tick “Remember this network” when giving one to your board. They are kept in your device’s secure system storage (the Keychain on iPhone, iPad and Mac): encrypted by the system, readable by Belksiri Scan only, and not synchronised to your other devices. Settings › Clear stored preferences deletes them. Passwords an earlier version kept in the application’s preferences are moved to the secure storage, then removed from the preferences.
  • A troubleshooting file, only when a reply from your board cannot be read: the raw reply is saved in the application’s private folder. It is never sent anywhere.

An image keeps whatever its file contains. A photo chosen from your library may carry metadata, such as where it was taken; the application stores the image as it is and includes it as it is in a project file you export.

Deleting the application deletes this data. A backup of your device made by iCloud or your computer is between you and that service.

Windows. What this section says about the Keychain, deleting the application and iCloud concerns iPhone, iPad and Mac. On a Windows PC, the Wi-Fi passwords you ask to remember are kept encrypted in a file of the application, with a key held in Windows Credential Manager for your Windows user account on this PC; the application does not synchronise them. This protection belongs to your Windows user account: unlike the Keychain, it does not reserve them for Belksiri Scan, and another program running under your account could reach them. Settings › Clear stored preferences deletes them; use it before uninstalling if you want them gone. Files you have exported stay where you saved them. Uninstalling may leave your projects and settings in place: they are in the folder %APPDATA%\Belksiri\Belksiri Scan of your user account, which you can delete yourself.

B. Permissions

iPhone, iPad and Mac.

  • Bluetooth: to find and talk to your ESP32-C5 board.
  • Location (Mac only): macOS requires it before an application can list nearby Wi-Fi networks or read the name of the network the Mac is on. Belksiri Scan never requests, reads or stores your position. On iPhone and iPad the application does not ask for location.
  • Local network (iPhone and iPad): to measure the round trip to your router.
  • Camera and photo library: only to take or choose a floor plan.
  • Files and folders: only those you choose, to open, save or export a project or a report.
  • USB serial port (Mac only): to install firmware on your board through its USB cable, with the flashing tool included in the application. It runs only during an installation you start, within the application’s sandbox, and stops when the application quits.

You can refuse or withdraw any of them in your device’s settings; only the feature that needs it stops working.

Windows.

  • Location: Windows can require your location consent (Settings › Privacy & security › Location) before an application can list nearby Wi-Fi networks or read the access point the PC is connected to. It may then show its own prompt and indicate that the application is using location. The Microsoft Store package declares this capability for that reason. Belksiri Scan does not call the Windows location service, and does not read or store your position.
  • Bluetooth: only to find and talk to an optional ESP32-C5 board.
  • Network: Internet and local network access, for the tests in section D, including the round trip to your router.
  • Files and folders: only those you choose, to open, save or export a project or a report. A floor plan is an image file you choose; the application does not use the camera on Windows.
  • USB serial port: to install firmware on your board through its USB cable, with the flashing tool included in the application. It runs only to detect the board and during an installation you start.

If you refuse location consent, Wi-Fi scanning by the PC, and what depends on it, stops working; Ping does not need it.

C. Your ESP32-C5 board

The board is your own device. The application sends it, over Bluetooth Low Energy or USB: commands (scan, measure, restart), the name and password of the Wi-Fi network you ask it to join, and firmware updates. The Bluetooth link of the current firmware is not encrypted, and the board stores the network name and password without encryption: anyone within Bluetooth range with suitable tools could read or control it. Use it on networks and in places where that is acceptable to you.

The board connects to the Internet only to run the tests you start (for example a ping to an address you choose).

Windows. On a Windows PC the board is optional, because the PC scans Wi-Fi itself. If you use one, this section applies to it.

D. Network tests and the services they contact

These tests are started by you. They send no personal information, no identifier and no measurement result: only the traffic the test needs. The services receive your public IP address and the technical data of any connection, and process them under their own policies.

  • Ping: from this device, timed connection attempts (TCP, port 443 then 80, no data sent) to 1.1.1.1 (Cloudflare), 8.8.8.8 (Google), 9.9.9.9 (Quad9), your router, or an address you type; when your ESP32-C5 board is the measurement source, the board itself sends ICMP echo requests to the same target. A name you type is first resolved by your usual DNS service. The Health diagnostic and Survey “Full” points use only this ping; when the Health diagnostic’s ping went to your router, it adds at most three attempts to 1.1.1.1 (Cloudflare) to check that a target outside your local network answers.
  • Full diagnostic: a DNS lookup of example.com, a connection to 1.1.1.1 and to your router.

Policies of these services: Cloudflare, Google, Quad9. Belksiri does not receive the results of any test.

E. Buying Belksiri Scan Pro

iPhone, iPad and Mac. The purchase takes place entirely within Apple’s App Store. Belksiri never sees your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. To unlock Pro, the application asks Apple’s StoreKit, on your device, whether this Apple Account owns Belksiri Scan Pro, and receives a transaction signed by Apple (product, transaction number, date). That record stays on your device; it is not sent to Belksiri. Apple processes your purchase under its own privacy policy.

Windows. The purchase takes place entirely within the Microsoft Store, as an add-on to the application. Belksiri never sees your payment details, your name or your Microsoft account. To unlock Pro, the application asks the Microsoft Store, on your PC, whether the licence for the Belksiri Scan Pro add-on is active. Nothing about it is sent to Belksiri. Microsoft processes your purchase under its own privacy statement.

F. Exports and sharing

When you export a report or a project, or share it, the file goes where you choose: a folder, Files, iCloud Drive, an e-mail, another application. Reports and projects contain network names, access point identifiers and measurements. What happens to them afterwards depends on where you sent them.

G. What is never done

No advertising. No analytics or usage statistics. No crash reports sent. No sale or sharing of personal data. No profile. No account.

H. Children

Belksiri Scan is a technical tool not aimed at children. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

I. Your rights

Because Belksiri holds no personal data from the application, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete: you control everything on your device. If you write to us, we use your message and address only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed. You may contact us with any question, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).
