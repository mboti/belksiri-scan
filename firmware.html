Preparing the ESP32-C5 board

On iPhone and iPad, Belksiri Scan measures with an ESP32-C5 board. To install its firmware, connect the board over USB to a computer and use Belksiri Firmware Installer, a free standalone utility: no Belksiri Scan, account, Internet connection or developer tool is needed on that computer.

State of this version

  • Pre-release for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel). The Windows version is not available yet.
  • Not yet validated on a real board: tested with automated tests on simulated boards and by starting the application. The USB installation engine is the one Belksiri Scan uses on Mac.
  • Ad-hoc signed, not notarised by Apple: macOS asks for permission the first time you open it (see below).

Requirements

  • macOS 12 or later.
  • An ESP32-C5 board with at least 4 MB of flash. Any other chip, or a smaller or unreadable flash, is refused before anything is written.
  • A USB data cable: many cables only charge.
  • USB driver: none for the ESP32-C5’s native USB port. Boards with a CP210x or CH340 bridge need a driver, from that chip’s maker, on Windows only.

Included firmware

ESP Device Studio 1.0.4 for ESP32-C5 (ESP-IDF v5.5.4). SHA-256 of the image: 83de9994a710dba224c7ccb0248bb4e2334af38ce3e32e818c5d0cba66a9a499, checked by the utility before writing. It bundles the espflash 4.5.0 flashing tool (MIT and Apache-2.0 licences), unmodified.

Opening the application the first time

  1. Open the .dmg file and drag Belksiri Firmware Installer to Applications.
  2. Open the application. macOS says it could not be verified: click Done.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, click Open Anyway next to Belksiri Firmware Installer, and confirm.

This permission covers this application only. Do not turn off your Mac’s protections.

Installing the firmware

  1. Connect the ESP32-C5 board to the computer with a USB data cable.
  2. Check the board line: ESP32-C5 · 4 MB flash · Compatible. If several boards are connected, choose one.
  3. Click Install firmware and confirm.
  4. Wait for Firmware installed and verified. Do not unplug the board or close the window while it runs.
  5. On the iPhone or iPad, open Belksiri Scan and connect to the board over Bluetooth.

What is erased: the settings stored on the board, including its Wi-Fi network and password. The confirmation dialog says so. “Verified” means the board was read back and matches the image; connecting from Belksiri Scan then confirms the firmware runs.

If the board does not respond

Hold the BOOT button, press and release RESET, release BOOT, then click Try again. On an error, Copy details copies a report to include in your message to mboti.dev@gmail.com.
