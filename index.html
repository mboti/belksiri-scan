Belksiri Scan

Measure. Map. Diagnose.

Wi-Fi scanning, network tests and coverage surveys on your floor plans, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Discover Belksiri Scan Privacy Policy

Features

From the air around you to a report you can hand over

  • Scan

    The networks around you, with their access points (BSSID), signal, channel, band and security, and the manufacturer of each access point from a database built into the application.

  • Diagnose

    Ping your router, a public resolver or any address; follow latency, jitter and packet loss; compare your local network with a public target.

  • Survey

    Import a floor plan, place your access points and measure point by point. Coverage maps show where the signal is strong and where it is not.

  • Your ESP32-C5 board

    Configure it over Bluetooth and use it as a measurement source. On a Mac, install its firmware over USB.

  • Belksiri Scan Pro

    Several floors and access points per project, the survey analysis, the project report export and the Health diagnostic. A one-time purchase, with no subscription.

  • Your data stays yours

    No account, no advertising, no analytics. Projects stay on your device; network tests contact public services only when you use them.

Platforms

Available for

One purchase of Pro covers iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account. On iPhone and iPad, iOS does not let applications scan Wi-Fi networks: scanning and survey measurements use the ESP32-C5 board. On a Mac, the Mac itself can scan.
